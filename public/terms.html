03Event planning services

Enquiries and proposals

When you send us an enquiry or book a consultation, we will discuss your event and, where it fits, send you a written proposal. The proposal sets out the scope of work, our fee, the deposit, payment dates and anything specific to your event. A proposal is valid for 14 days unless it says otherwise.

Confirming your booking

Your booking is confirmed when you have accepted the proposal (in writing, including by email or WhatsApp) and we have received the deposit. We cannot hold a date until both have happened.

Payments

  • All fees are in Nigerian Naira (₦) unless the proposal states another currency.
  • The balance of our fee must be paid by the date shown in your proposal, and in full before the event day unless we have agreed otherwise in writing.
  • Where we pay vendors on your behalf, you must provide those funds before we commit to the vendor.
  • If a payment is late, we may pause work on your event until it is received. We are not responsible for vendors or venues that are lost because of a late payment.

Changes to your event

You can ask to change your event, such as the guest count, venue, date or services. We will tell you if the change affects the fee or the timeline. Changes are confirmed once we agree them in writing.

Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate information and make decisions in good time so we can keep to the plan.
  • Get any permits, approvals or permissions that only you can obtain, unless our proposal says we will handle them.
  • Make sure your guests behave safely and follow venue rules. You are responsible for any damage caused by you or your guests.

04Cancellations and postponements

Unless your proposal or contract sets different terms, the following applies.

If you cancel

  • You must tell us in writing.
  • The deposit is non-refundable, because it covers the planning time and dates we reserve for you.
  • Any other payments you have made to us for our fee will be refunded, minus the value of the work we have already done.
  • Money already paid to vendors and venues is subject to their own cancellation terms. We will help you recover what we can, but we cannot guarantee refunds from third parties.

If you postpone

If you move your event to a new date, we will try to transfer your deposit and payments to the new date, subject to our availability and the terms of the vendors and venue involved. Extra costs caused by the change are payable by you.

If we cancel

If we have to cancel for a reason within our control, we will refund all payments made to us for our fee and help you find another planner where possible.

  • where a supplier lets us down, we will do our best to find a replacement and keep your event on track.

Registration and payment

  • You register on the Training page and pay the tuition through our payment partner, Flutterwave. Your place is confirmed once payment is successful.
  • The tuition shown on the Website at the time you pay is the price you pay. We may change tuition for future cohorts.
  • Referral or discount codes must be valid when used and cannot be exchanged for cash.

How the training runs

The training is delivered 100% online through live, instructor-led virtual classes. You are responsible for having a suitable device and internet connection. Where we provide recordings, templates or other materials, they are for your personal learning only.

We may change the class timetable, instructors or course content where needed. If we do, we will tell you in advance where we can.

Joining after classes have started

If you enrol after a cohort has started, you can join the remaining live classes and catch up using the recordings and materials we make available. Late enrolment does not extend the cohort's end date.

Refunds

  • You can request a full refund within 7 days of paying, as long as you have attended no more than one live class.
  • After that, tuition is non-refundable. You can instead ask to transfer once to a future cohort, subject to available places.
  • If we cancel a cohort, you can choose a full refund or a place on the next cohort.
  • Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 14 working days. Fees charged by Flutterwave or your bank may not be recoverable.

Certification

A Mara Academy certificate is issued to students who complete the programme requirements set out during the course, such as attendance and assignments. The certificate shows that you have completed our training. It is not a government or university qualification.

Conduct

We expect students to be respectful to instructors and other students. We may remove anyone from a class, without a refund, for harassment, disruption, or for sharing course materials or class links with people who have not enrolled.
