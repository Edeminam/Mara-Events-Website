Corporate events that run to the minute

Abuja is where government agencies, embassies, development organisations and company head offices meet. Events here often bring together senior officials, partners and press, and they carry real expectations around protocol, punctuality and presentation. A late start, a failed microphone or a power cut is noticed by everyone in the room.

Mara Events plans and manages corporate events from the first brief to the final report. We find the venue, manage registration and seating, build the stage and AV plan with proper power backup, brief every vendor and run the programme on the day, so your team can focus on guests, speakers and sponsors.

We have been the official event planner for the Africa International Conference on Clean Energy and Energy Storage (AICCEES) for four consecutive editions. For larger programmes with delegates, speakers and exhibitors, see our conference planning service.

How our fee works

Most of the cost of an event is the event itself: venue, catering, décor, sound and logistics. Our fee is separate and usually works out at 10 to 20% of the total budget, or a flat fee for day-of coordination and simpler events.

On a ₦5M event, that is about ₦500K to ₦1M. We agree the fee and what it covers in writing before we start, and we recommend setting aside another 10 to 15% as a contingency for fuel surcharges, extra catering and last-minute changes.

FAQ

Corporate event questions

Straight answers to the questions clients ask us most.

How much does corporate event management cost in Abuja?

Most corporate events in Nigeria cost between ₦500K for a small meeting and ₦25M or more for a large launch or summit, depending on guest numbers, venue and production. Our management fee is usually 10 to 20% of the total budget. We give you a written quote after the brief.

How far in advance should we start planning?

Eight to twelve weeks is comfortable for most corporate events. Large galas, launches and conferences with sponsors or international guests benefit from three to six months.

Which Abuja venues do you recommend?

It depends on your guest numbers and format. Our guide to the best corporate venues in Abuja compares capacities and rental ranges for the main hotels and conference centres.

Can you run hybrid or live-streamed events?

Yes. We can add streaming, a virtual audience and a production crew to an in-person event. Our article on hybrid events in Nigeria explains the options.

Do you manage events outside Abuja?

Yes. We plan corporate events across Nigeria, including Lagos.
