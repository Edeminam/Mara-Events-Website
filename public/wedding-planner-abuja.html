Planning a Nigerian wedding, without the stress

A Nigerian wedding is rarely one event. There may be an introduction, a traditional ceremony, a church or court wedding and a reception, sometimes on different days and at different venues, with two families and hundreds of guests to look after. Every family has its own customs and its own idea of how the day should flow.

We have planned weddings and celebrations since 2020, and we build each plan around the way your families want to celebrate. That might be a white wedding with a cold-smoke first dance, a traditional ceremony with a monogrammed dance floor, or a military wedding with a sword-arch guard of honour. You get one team that keeps every vendor, payment, timeline and guest detail in one place.

We are based in Abuja and work across the city’s hotel ballrooms, event centres and marquee venues. We also plan weddings in Lagos and other cities, so families spread across Nigeria can still have one planner for the whole celebration.

How our fee works

Most of the cost of an event is the event itself: venue, catering, décor, sound and logistics. Our fee is separate and usually works out at 10 to 20% of the total budget, or a flat fee for day-of coordination and simpler events.

On a ₦5M event, that is about ₦500K to ₦1M. We agree the fee and what it covers in writing before we start, and we recommend setting aside another 10 to 15% as a contingency for fuel surcharges, extra catering and last-minute changes.

Weddings we have planned

Three celebrations from our portfolio, each with a very different look and feel.

Military wedding

A guard of honour under candle light

Military weddings follow a strict programme. Officers in ceremonial uniform formed a sword arch for the couple’s entrance, and a guard of honour lined a green-lit aisle beneath hanging candle lights, with an illuminated-sword salute as the couple walked through.

Moments like these depend on timing between the officers, the MC, the lighting and the photographers, so the entrance happens once and is captured properly.

Traditional wedding

Tosin and Kayode’s traditional wedding

Tosin and Kayode celebrated in matching royal-blue aso-oke, with a custom monogrammed dance floor bearing their names and a floral stage in white and blue.

Traditional ceremonies are full of moments for the families, from the entrance to the dances. The décor kept the couple’s colours consistent from the stage to the floor, so every photograph tells the same story.

White wedding reception

A cold-smoke first dance

For this reception, the first dance happened under a gold-framed floral stage, with low-lying cold smoke across the floor and stage sparklers to close the moment.

Effects like cold smoke and sparklers need the venue’s approval, safe placement and a cue sheet shared with the DJ and photographers.

Wedding planning questions

How much does a wedding planner cost in Abuja?

Our fee is usually 10 to 20% of your total wedding budget for full planning, or a flat fee for day-of coordination. The total cost of the wedding itself depends mostly on your guest count, venue and catering. We give you a written quote after the first consultation.

How early should we book a wedding planner?

For full planning, 9 to 12 months before the wedding gives you the widest choice of venues and vendors, especially for popular dates such as December. For day-of coordination, contact us at least six to eight weeks before.

Do you plan traditional weddings as well as white weddings?

Yes. We plan introductions, traditional ceremonies, white weddings and receptions, and many couples ask us to handle several of them as one plan with one budget.

Can we use vendors we have already booked?

Yes. Our hybrid package is built for this: we coordinate the vendors you already have and book only the ones you still need.

Do you plan weddings outside Abuja?

Yes. We plan weddings in Abuja, Lagos and other cities across Nigeria. See events in Lagos for how we plan there.

Will you be at the wedding on the day?

Yes. On every package our team is on site, running set-up, timings, vendors and guests, so you and your families can enjoy the day.
