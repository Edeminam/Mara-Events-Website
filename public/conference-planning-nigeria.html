One team for every moving part

A conference is many events at once. There is registration and accreditation, a plenary stage, panel sessions, breakout rooms, an exhibition floor, sponsor commitments, meals for hundreds of delegates and speakers arriving from different cities, and sometimes an online audience too. Each part depends on the others running on time.

Mara Events plans conferences, summits and seminars across Nigeria. We have been the official event planner for the Africa International Conference on Clean Energy and Energy Storage (AICCEES) for four consecutive editions, from stage design and delegate registration to exhibitions, panels and certificate presentations.

Whether you are running a first edition or the tenth, we can manage the whole conference or take on the parts your team does not have time for, such as registration, speakers’ logistics or the exhibition.

A typical conference timeline

Large conferences go best with four to six months of planning. Smaller seminars can be done in eight to ten weeks.

4 to 6 months out

Brief, budget, venue booking, date confirmed, sponsor packages and the first speaker invitations.

3 months out

Programme drafted, registration opens, exhibitor and sponsor sales, key vendors booked.

6 weeks out

Speakers confirmed with travel, stage and branding designed, catering and AV finalised.

Final week

Badges printed, run-of-show issued, technical rehearsal and venue walk-through.

Conference days

Our team runs registration, the stage, sessions, exhibitors and delegates, then wraps up with a summary.

How our fee works

Most of the cost of an event is the event itself: venue, catering, décor, sound and logistics. Our fee is separate and usually works out at 10 to 20% of the total budget, or a flat fee for day-of coordination and simpler events.

On a ₦5M event, that is about ₦500K to ₦1M. We agree the fee and what it covers in writing before we start, and we recommend setting aside another 10 to 15% as a contingency for fuel surcharges, extra catering and last-minute changes.

Conference case study

AICCEES, the Africa International Conference on Clean Energy and Energy Storage.

Registration and stage

AICCEES 2025: from the front desk to the plenary

The 3rd edition of AICCEES was held in 2025 at the Institute of Petroleum Studies. Delegates signed in at a staffed registration desk before moving into a full plenary hall.

The stage carried the conference identity and sponsor branding, with a lectern for keynotes and a panel set for discussions, and floor lettering that made the stage instantly recognisable in photographs.

“For the past four years, Mara Events has delivered AICCEES with professionalism, excellence, and seamless execution, contributing significantly to the success of every edition.”Engr. Anthony Akpasoh, Convener, Africa International Conference on Clean Energy & Energy Storage (AICCEES)

Sponsors were visible across a step-and-repeat wall used for guest photos and media interviews, and on the stage set itself.

FAQ

Conference planning questions

Straight answers to the questions clients ask us most.

How much does it cost to organise a conference in Nigeria?

A mid-size conference for 100 to 200 delegates typically costs ₦3M to ₦8M all-in, and a large conference for 300 to 500 delegates ₦10M to ₦25M. Venue, catering and AV are the biggest costs. Our planning fee is usually 10 to 20% of the total budget.

How far ahead should we start planning a conference?

Four to six months for a large conference with sponsors, exhibitors and travelling speakers. Smaller seminars can be planned in eight to ten weeks.

Can you manage sponsors and exhibitors?

Yes. We handle sponsor branding, step-and-repeat walls, exhibition stands and exhibitor logistics, so what was promised in each sponsor package is delivered on the day.

Do you handle delegate registration?

Yes. We can set up registration, print badges, run the registration desk and keep attendance records for your reports.

Which cities do you plan conferences in?

We are based in Abuja and plan conferences across Nigeria, including Lagos.

Can the conference be hybrid?

Yes. We can stream sessions to an online audience and manage speakers who join remotely, alongside the in-person programme.
