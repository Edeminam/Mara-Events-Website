Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this training program for?

This masterclass is designed for beginners who want to break into the event planning industry, intermediates looking to structure their operations, and business owners aiming to scale their luxury weddings and corporate event bookings.

The training is 100% virtual and runs over 6 weeks, starting 30 October 2026. There are no in-person sessions. You join live interactive online classes, real-time Q&As and digital planning workshops, and get access to recorded replays and course templates.
