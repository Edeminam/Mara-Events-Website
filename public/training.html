Frequently Asked Questions

When does Cohort 2 of the Mara Academy training start?

Cohort 2 starts on Monday, January 11, 2027. Registration is open now, and seats are confirmed once tuition is paid. Register early to secure your place before the cohort fills up.

Can I take this event planning course from anywhere in Nigeria?

Yes. All classes are held live online, so you can join from Abuja, Lagos, Port Harcourt, Kano or anywhere else in Nigeria or abroad. You only need a phone or laptop and a stable internet connection.

Who is this training program for?

This masterclass is designed for beginners who want to break into the event planning industry, intermediates looking to structure their operations, and business owners aiming to scale their luxury weddings and corporate event bookings.

What is the schedule and format of the classes?

Cohort 2 starts on January 11, 2027 and runs for 6 weeks. The training is 100% virtual, with no in-person sessions. You join live interactive online classes, real-time Q&As and digital planning workshops, and get access to recorded replays and course templates.

Tuition is ₦80,000 NGN. Enter your phone number in our registration form and click "Register & Pay". You will be redirected to our secure payment partner, Flutterwave, where you can safely pay via card, bank transfer, or USSD.

Is there a certificate awarded upon completion?

Yes, a Certificate of Completion credited by Mara Events will be awarded to all trainees who complete the 6 modules and successfully submit their Week 6 Capstone Project.
