Marketing Intern

Internship Unpaid Marketing
Apply by email

Help tell the Mara Events story. You will support our marketing team across social media, content and campaigns for our events, training programmes and blog.

What you'll do

  • Plan and post content for our social media channels
  • Help capture photos and short videos at events
  • Support campaigns for Mara Academy and our webinars
  • Track engagement and share simple weekly reports

Who we're looking for

  • A strong interest in marketing, media or events
  • Good written English and an eye for visuals
  • Comfortable with Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn
  • Organised, reliable and eager to learn

How to apply: send your CV to info@mara-events.com with the subject line "Application: Marketing Intern".
