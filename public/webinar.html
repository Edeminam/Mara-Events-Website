The conversation

AI is changing event planning. Here's where the line is.

Planners are hearing two stories: that AI will do everything, or that it's all hype. The truth sits in between, and knowing where is a real advantage.

What technology can handle

  • First drafts of proposals, run-of-shows and timelines
  • Guest lists, RSVPs, reminders and follow-ups
  • Budget tracking and vendor quote comparisons
  • Promotion copy, social posts and attendee FAQs
vs

What still needs a human in the room

  • Reading what a client really wants, beyond the brief
  • Negotiating with vendors, venues and partners
  • Making the call when plans fall apart on the day
  • Creating the atmosphere guests remember
