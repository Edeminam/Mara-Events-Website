About the webinar

AI in event planning: where technology helps, and where people still lead

Event planners are hearing two stories: that AI will do everything, or that it is all hype. The truth sits in between. In this free webinar, the Mara Events team walks through how AI tools and event technology are changing the work of event planners in Nigeria, and where human judgement still makes the difference.

Whether you plan weddings, corporate events or conferences in Abuja, Lagos or beyond, you will leave with a clear view of what to automate and which skills to keep sharpening.

Hosted by Mara Events, official event planner for AICCEES and organisers of 200+ events since 2021.

What event technology can handle

  • First drafts of proposals, run-of-shows and timelines
  • Guest lists, RSVPs, reminders and follow-ups
  • Budget tracking and vendor quote comparisons
  • Promotion copy, social posts and attendee FAQs

What still needs a human in the room

  • Reading what a client really wants, beyond the brief
  • Negotiating with vendors, venues and partners
  • Making the call when plans fall apart on the day
  • Creating the atmosphere guests remember
