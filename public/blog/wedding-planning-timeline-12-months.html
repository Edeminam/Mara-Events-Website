Wedding Planning Timeline: 12 Months to Your Big Day

Emmanuel Brendan, Event Manager September 14, 2020 10 min read
  • Official event planner for AICCEES, 4 consecutive editions
  • 200+ events delivered since 2020
  • Events across Abuja & Lagos

A Nigerian wedding is one of the most celebrated events in our culture, vibrant, joyful, and deeply meaningful. Planning one, however, can feel overwhelming without a clear roadmap. Whether you're planning a grand Abuja reception or an intimate Lagos garden wedding, this month-by-month timeline ensures nothing slips through the cracks.

📌 Note

This timeline assumes a 12-month engagement. If your timeline is shorter, compress the early months and prioritise venue, caterer, and photographer bookings above all else, these book up fastest.

12 Months Before: The Foundation

  • Set your overall budget and agree on who is contributing (families, couple)
  • Define your guest list size, this drives almost every other decision
  • Discuss your wedding vision: style, theme, indoor vs. outdoor
  • Start researching venues in your preferred city (Abuja, Lagos, or destination)
  • Visit 3 to 5 shortlisted venues and request quotations
  • Secure your venue booking, put down the deposit immediately after deciding
  • Hire your wedding planner if using one (this is the #1 thing to book first)

10 to 11 Months Before: The Key Vendors

Popular vendors in Nigeria book up 9 to 12 months in advance for peak wedding season (November to January and May to August). Don't delay these bookings:

  • Book your photographer and videographer; review full portfolio, not just highlights
  • Secure your caterer; request a tasting before finalising
  • Book your decorator/stylist and agree on a mood board
  • Research and shortlist DJs, bands, or live performers
  • Start bridal outfit shopping (Nigerian bridal fashion often requires 4 to 6 months for custom pieces)
  • Begin engagement/pre-wedding photoshoot planning if desired

8 to 9 Months Before: The Traditional & Legal

  • Plan your traditional engagement/introduction ceremony separately if applicable
  • Visit your state registry or marriage registry, understand the documentation required
  • Begin assembling required documents (birth certificates, identity cards, passport photos, etc.)
  • Book your MC (Master of Ceremonies), great MCs book even faster than photographers
  • Finalise your wedding colours and share with all vendors
  • Plan the bridal party, officially ask your bridal train

6 to 7 Months Before: Styling & Invitations

  • Design and order invitations (print and digital)
  • Book the cake designer, request a tasting and confirm design
  • Finalise décor concept and get a detailed quotation
  • Book transportation: couple's car, bridal party vehicles, and guest shuttles if needed
  • Begin planning the honeymoon, book flights and accommodation
  • Organise aso-ebi (family fabric/uniform) and distribute
  • Book hair and makeup artist, confirm trial date

4 to 5 Months Before: The Details

  • Send out formal invitations (physical or digital)
  • Set up a wedding website or WhatsApp group for guests
  • Plan and book the reception programme: order of events, speeches, special performances
  • Confirm all vendor bookings with written contracts
  • Plan the bridal shower and bachelor/bachelorette events
  • Book accommodation for out-of-town guests if needed
  • Order wedding favours (souvenirs)

2 to 3 Months Before: Final Fittings & Confirmations

  • Schedule bridal outfit fitting #1, give feedback for alterations
  • Confirm final guest count and share with caterer and venue
  • Finalise the wedding day timeline, minute by minute
  • Do a venue walk-through with your planner and key vendors
  • Confirm generator/power arrangements with venue
  • Confirm all vendor logistics: arrival times, setup windows, payment schedules

1 Month Before: The Final Push

  • Final bridal outfit fitting and accessories selection
  • Hair and makeup trial session
  • Confirm final guest numbers and submit to all vendors
  • Collect all marriage registry documents
  • Brief your bridal party on their roles and timeline
  • Prepare all vendor final payments
  • Do a final venue inspection with your planner

1 Week Before: Relax and Prepare

  • Confirm all vendor arrival times one final time via WhatsApp or call
  • Prepare wedding day emergency kit (safety pins, hair spray, stain remover, extra shoes)
  • Delegate on-the-day responsibilities to trusted family members or your planner
  • Rest, hydrate, and focus on being present
"The couples who enjoy their wedding day most are those who handed over the details to a trusted planner weeks in advance. Your job on the day is to be the couple; let us handle the chaos."Emmanuel Brendan, Event Manager
