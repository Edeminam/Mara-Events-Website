How Much Does It Really Cost to Plan a Corporate Event in Nigeria?

Mara Events Team July 27, 2026 9 min read

One of the most common questions we get at Mara Events is: "How much does a corporate event in Nigeria really cost?" The honest answer is: it depends on many factors, but that's not helpful to anyone trying to plan a budget.

This guide breaks down the real numbers across each major event category, based on our experience planning corporate events across Abuja and Lagos. These are actual market rates as of 2026.

📌 Disclaimer

All pricing figures are approximate ranges based on current market rates in Nigeria. Actual costs vary significantly based on event scale, guest count, date, and vendor quality tier.

1. Venue Rental: ₦800,000 to ₦8,000,000+

Venue is typically the largest single cost in a corporate event. In Nigeria, pricing varies based on:

  • Basic conference room (50 to 100 guests): ₦150,000 to ₦500,000/day
  • Mid-range hotel ballroom (100 to 300 guests): ₦800,000 to ₦2,500,000/day
  • Luxury 5-star hotel ballroom (300 to 500+ guests): ₦3,000,000 to ₦8,000,000/day
  • Outdoor venues (gardens, estates): ₦500,000 to ₦3,000,000 (structure setup adds ₦500K to ₦2M)

Note: Many hotels include basic chairs, tables, and AV equipment in the venue rental fee. Always ask what is included.

2. Catering: ₦6,000 to ₦25,000 per person

For a 200-person event, catering alone could range from ₦1.2M to ₦5M+.

3. Audio-Visual & Production: ₦300,000 to ₦3,000,000

  • Basic AV (projector, PA system, 2 mics): ₦150,000 to ₦350,000
  • Mid-range production (LED screens, live sound, lighting): ₦500,000 to ₦1,500,000
  • Premium production (LED walls, full lighting rig, live stream): ₦2,000,000 to ₦5,000,000+

4. Décor & Branding: ₦300,000 to ₦2,500,000

  • Basic branded backdrop + table settings: ₦200,000 to ₦500,000
  • Full floral and branded décor: ₦800,000 to ₦2,000,000
  • Premium immersive décor: ₦2,500,000+

5. Photography & Videography: ₦200,000 to ₦1,500,000

  • Single photographer (half-day): ₦80,000 to ₦200,000
  • Photo + video team (full day): ₦300,000 to ₦800,000
  • Premium multi-camera + drone + same-day edit: ₦1,000,000 to ₦2,500,000

6. MC & Entertainment: ₦150,000 to ₦2,000,000+

  • Professional MC: ₦100,000 to ₦500,000
  • Live band (3 to 5 piece): ₦300,000 to ₦1,000,000
  • A-list comedian/performer: ₦1,000,000 to ₦5,000,000+

7. Event Management (Professional Planner): ₦200,000 to ₦1,500,000

Event management fees in Nigeria typically range from 10 to 20% of the total event budget, or a flat fee based on complexity. For a ₦5M event budget, expect a management fee of ₦500K to ₦1M.

Always Include a Contingency

Add 10 to 15% to any event budget as a contingency fund. In Nigeria's event landscape, last-minute costs are virtually guaranteed: generator fuel surcharges, extra catering portions, emergency vendor replacements, and logistics overruns are common. A contingency fund prevents panic decisions.
